## UX Designer

**Role Summary:** Designs user experiences and interfaces, focusing on usability and accessibility.
**Responsibilities:** 
- Create wireframes and prototypes
- Validate user flows with PM and Developers
- Ensure requirements for usability and accessibility are met
**Typical Communication:** Weekly design reviews, sprint planning with PM/PdM, ad-hoc feedback with Developers

## DevOps Engineer

**Role Summary:** Automates deployments, maintains pipelines and infrastructure.
**Responsibilities:**
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Monitor releases & coordinate incident response
- Liaise with QA for release readiness

## Business Analyst

**Role Summary:** Bridges business needs and delivery planning.
**Responsibilities:** 
- Gather detailed requirements
- Write user stories and acceptance criteria
- Support backlog refinement with PM/PdM

## Documentation Specialist / Technical Writer

**Role Summary:** Maintains and organizes project documentation.
**Responsibilities:** 
- Document processes, APIs, onboarding materials
- Collaborate with Developers, PMs and PdMs
